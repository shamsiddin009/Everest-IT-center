# EVEREST ACADEMY — BRAND GUIDELINES

## 1. Brand Essence
- **Name:** Everest Academy (Everst)
- **Tagline:** *"Bilim bilan kelajagingizni quring"* (Build your future with knowledge)
- **Mission:** Transforming youth and ambitious learners into world-class innovators through elite, structured education in IT, Languages, Mathematics, and Computer Literacy.

## 2. Core Archetype & Personality
- **Primary Archetype:** The Sage + The Explorer
- **Personality Traits:**
  - **Innovative:** Cutting-edge teaching tools, modern labs, high-tech aesthetic.
  - **Ambitious:** Aiming for highest peaks (Mount Everest metaphor).
  - **Trustworthy:** Verified curriculum, structured syllabi, transparent learning outcomes.
  - **Youthful & Future-Oriented:** Dynamic, inspirational, electric dark-mode visual signature.

## 3. Brand Identity & Visual Language
- **Brandmark Symbol:** Ascending crystalline mountain peak interwoven with futuristic digital geometry and electric energy nodes.
- **Color Codes:**
  - **Midnight Navy (`#080F1A`):** Infinite potential, depth, seriousness.
  - **Deep Surface (`#0D1730`):** Foundational architecture, stability.
  - **Elevated Surface (`#111F3D`):** Interactive card elevation.
  - **Electric Indigo (`#655BFF`):** Wisdom, intellect, innovation.
  - **Electric Cyan (`#00C9E8`):** Energy, clarity, future direction.
  - **Text Primary (`#F4F7FF`):** Maximum readability, pristine contrast.
  - **Text Secondary (`#A8B5D0`):** Subordinate metadata, balanced hierarchy.
  - **Subtle Border (`#253454`):** Refined technical definition.

## 4. Tone of Voice
- **Language:** Uzbek (Latin alphabet, correct orthography `o‘`, `g‘`, `sh`, `ch`).
- **Tone:** Inspiring, encouraging, direct, and professional without false claims or fake numbers.
